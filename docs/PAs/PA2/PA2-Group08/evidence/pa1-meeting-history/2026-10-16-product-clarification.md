## Product decision

**Performed by:** Hoàng Cao Phong | **Reviewed by:** Võ Mỹ Ngọc | **Edited by:** Hoàng Cao Phong

The group adopted DineVina as a **B2B2C restaurant discovery, table reservation, and event booking marketplace**. Customers browse and request tables or events; restaurant partners publish venue information and handle requests; the platform administers the marketplace. The partner dashboard supports this workflow. A full POS, inventory, accounting, or restaurant management system is outside the PA1 scope. The earlier shorthand in the draft notes is superseded by this description, consistent with Ngọc's proposal and `docs/product-scope.md`.

For PA1, the group selected **nine** functional groups from Kiên's larger candidate set. Notifications support booking and event flows, while ratings and reviews are deferred. The first planned AI capability is an explainable restaurant recommendation based on partner-verified venue data. RAG assistance, menu OCR, and visual search remain later candidates. These are scope decisions for the proposal, not claims of implemented features.
