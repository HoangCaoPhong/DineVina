
# 4. Team Contract
## 4.1. Team Roles and Responsibilities

The DineVina team consists of five members following the Scrum process. Each member has a primary responsibility while participating in full-stack development, including frontend, backend, testing, and documentation.

| Member | Primary Role | Main Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Hoàng Cao Phong | Team Leader / Scrum Master; AI Integration Lead | Coordinate team activities, organize Scrum meetings, monitor progress, and lead AI integration. |
| Võ Mỹ Ngọc | Product Owner; UI/UX Lead | Manage product requirements and backlog, define acceptance criteria, coordinate UI/UX design, and support frontend development. |
| Lương Thiện Nhân | Data Management Lead | Organize restaurant data, maintain data consistency, and support database development and AI data preparation. |
| Nguyễn Trung Kiên | Backend Lead | Coordinate API development, implement booking logic, manage database operations, and support system integration. |
| Trương Khánh Duy | AI Development Lead | Develop and evaluate AI recommendation features and support their integration into the system. |

All members must contribute to frontend and backend development, participate in project discussions, review teammates' work, and complete assigned tasks. Primary roles define leadership responsibilities but do not limit participation in other areas.

## 4.2. Communication Plan

The team uses the following tools:

- **Zalo:** Daily communication, discussions, announcements, and urgent issue reporting.
- **Trello:** Task assignment, deadline management, and progress tracking.
- **Git/GitHub:** Source code and documentation version control.
- **Moodle:** Course announcements and assignment submissions.

**Communication Rules:**

- Members should check Zalo regularly and respond to important messages within one working day.
- Members must notify the team in advance if they cannot attend a meeting.
- Important decisions and meeting outcomes must be documented.
- Members must update their assigned tasks on Trello and report difficulties promptly.
- All discussions should remain respectful and constructive.

**Meeting Schedule:**

Each sprint includes one Sprint Planning meeting, two Scrum meetings, and one Sprint Review meeting.

Meeting dates and times will be arranged flexibly through Zalo based on members' availability and project progress. The team will agree on suitable meeting times in advance, ensuring that all required meetings are conducted within each sprint.

## 4.3. Work Schedule and Deadlines

The project follows Scrum with **five sprints**, each corresponding to a Project Assignment (PA) and lasting approximately 2–3 weeks.

| Sprint | Assignment | Main Objectives |
|---|---|---|
| Sprint 1 | PA1 | Project proposal, existing app survey, team contract, and tool setup. |
| Sprint 2 | PA2 | Project plan, vision document, and Spec Kit initialization. |
| Sprint 3 | PA3 | Use-case specifications, UI prototypes, and implementation of one functional group. |
| Sprint 4 | PA4 | Software architecture and implementation of two additional functional groups. |
| Sprint 5 | PA5 | Complete remaining features, conduct testing, prepare final documentation, and demonstrate the finished system. |

The team will follow official assignment deadlines announced through Moodle. Detailed task deadlines will be managed on Trello.

**Task and Deadline Rules:**

1. All tasks, including coding, documentation, testing, and self-training, must be recorded on Trello before work begins.
2. Each task must have exactly one responsible member and a separate reviewer.
3. Task records must include creation, assignment, and actual completion dates, along with the agreed deadline.
4. Members must update task progress regularly and complete assigned work on time.
5. If delays occur, the responsible member must notify the team, explain the issue, and propose a revised deadline.
6. The team may adjust schedules or reassign unfinished tasks when necessary.

Members will coordinate their availability through Zalo. Work sessions and meetings may be adjusted to accommodate academic schedules and unexpected circumstances, provided that project deadlines and required deliverables are maintained.

## 4.4. Code and Documentation Standards

**Coding Standards:**

- Use Git/GitHub for version control and maintain a private repository.
- Follow consistent naming conventions, formatting, and folder organization.
- Write readable, maintainable code and add comments when necessary.
- Develop tasks on separate branches and have changes reviewed by another member before merging.
- Never commit passwords, API keys, or sensitive information.

The team plans to use React for frontend development and FastAPI for backend development. Other technologies will be determined during development.

**Testing Standards:**

- Members must test their implemented features before submitting them for review.
- Frontend and backend integration must be verified.
- Bugs must be recorded, assigned, and fixed.
- Functional test cases and results must be documented as required in PA5.

**Documentation Standards:**

- All project documents must be written in English using Markdown.
- Diagrams must use Mermaid where required.
- Documentation must be version-controlled and updated when changes occur.
- The team will use Spec Kit for specification-driven development from PA2 onward.
- AI tool usage must be documented according to course requirements.

## 4.5. Accountability and Performance

Each member is responsible for completing assigned tasks and contributing fairly to the project.

Contributions will be evaluated based on task completion, work quality, meeting participation, communication, collaboration, and documentation. Trello records, Git commit history, and completed deliverables will provide supporting evidence.

If a member fails to meet their responsibilities:

1. The team leader will discuss the issue privately with the member.
2. If the problem continues, the team will establish a corrective action plan with clear deadlines.
3. Repeated non-compliance may result in task reassignment and a report to the teaching assistant or instructor.

Members will have an opportunity to explain their circumstances before corrective actions are taken.

## 4.6. Decision-Making Process

The team will prioritize discussion and consensus when making decisions.

If consensus cannot be reached, decisions will be made by majority vote, requiring approval from at least three of the five members.

The Product Owner coordinates requirement priorities, while technical decisions are discussed among relevant members. Major changes to project scope, schedule, or architecture require team approval.

The team leader facilitates discussions and records final decisions but cannot override the agreed majority decision.

## 4.7. Conflict Resolution

Conflicts will be resolved through the following process:

1. Involved members discuss the issue directly and respectfully.
2. If unresolved, the team leader facilitates a group discussion.
3. The team agrees on a solution through consensus or majority vote.
4. If the conflict persists, the team seeks guidance from the teaching assistant or instructor.

All members must focus on constructive solutions rather than personal criticism.

## 4.8. Contract Review and Update Process

The Team Contract will be reviewed at the end of each sprint and remain applicable throughout PA1–PA5.

Any member may propose changes when project requirements, schedules, or team circumstances change. Changes must be discussed and approved by at least three of the five members, documented, and communicated through Zalo.

All members must review and agree to the initial contract before starting the project.

**Team Agreement**

By agreeing to this contract, all members commit to fulfilling their responsibilities, following the established rules, and collaborating throughout the development of DineVina.
