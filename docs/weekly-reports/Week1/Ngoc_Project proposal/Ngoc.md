### 3. Project proposal
## 3.1. Idea and Value

DineVina is a web-based platform designed to support restaurant discovery, reservations, and party booking management, focusing on small and medium-sized restaurants (SMEs).

Customers often need to compare restaurants based on location, guest capacity, budget, party type, menu, and availability before making a reservation. DineVina aims to simplify this process by bringing restaurant information and booking services into a single platform. Customers can search for suitable venues, explore menus and party packages, and make reservations or submit party booking requests.

For restaurant partners, DineVina provides a centralized workspace to manage restaurant information, menus, booking requests, customer records, and availability. The platform connects customer reservations with restaurant-side management rather than functioning solely as a restaurant listing website.

The initial release will focus on restaurant discovery and standard table reservations. Party booking management and AI-assisted recommendations will be developed as subsequent features once the core booking system and restaurant data are established.

## 3.2. Target Users and Environment

DineVina serves three main user groups:

- **Customers:** Individuals or groups looking for restaurants for regular dining or special occasions. They can search for restaurants, compare available options, view menus, and submit booking requests.
- **Restaurant Partners:** Small and medium-sized restaurants that register on the platform to manage their restaurant profiles, menus, availability, customer information, and reservations.
- **Platform Administrators:** Responsible for reviewing restaurant registrations and managing restaurant information on the platform.

DineVina will operate as a responsive web application accessible through desktop and mobile browsers. Both customers and restaurant partners will use the same platform through interfaces corresponding to their roles.

The system is planned to use React for frontend development and FastAPI for backend development. Other technologies will be determined during the development process based on the project's requirements.
