## Evidence boundary
**Performed by:** Hoàng Cao Phong | **Reviewed by:** Võ Mỹ Ngọc (assigned; review record not supplied) | **Edited by:** Hoàng Cao Phong

The package has substantive Plan and Vision content. The available sources do not include actual minutes for all four PA2 ceremonies, personal tutorial records for four members, complete usage declarations, or a final peer-review record. The relevant reports say exactly what the sources establish. Those records must be added after the corresponding activities occur, and the affected PDFs and ZIP must then be regenerated.
